Think's workspace tools are now `@cloudflare/computer`'s `createAITools`, configured with the new `workspaceTools` property; `createWorkspaceTools`, `workspaceBash`, and `@cloudflare/think/tools/workspace` are removed. See [Built-in workspace tools](https://github.com/cloudflare/agents/blob/main/docs/think/tools.md#built-in-workspace-tools).
